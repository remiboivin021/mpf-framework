# Assumptions and Scope

## Purpose

Document assumptions and scope boundaries for the safety case.

## Scope

### In Scope
- Robot motion control
- Safety systems
- Human-robot interaction
- Emergency stop
- Normal operation

### Out of Scope
- External infrastructure
- Third-party equipment
- Environmental hazards (natural disasters)
- Intentional misuse

## Assumptions

### Operating Environment
- Controlled indoor environment
- Temperature: 10-40°C
- Humidity: 20-80% non-condensing
- Trained operators present

### Human Factors
- Operators are trained and certified
- Safety procedures are followed
- Personal protective equipment (PPE) worn
- Emergency stop is accessible

### System Assumptions
- Regular maintenance performed
- Calibration maintained
- Software updated as required
- Hardware in good condition

### Safety System Assumptions
- Emergency stop functions correctly
- Watchdog system is operational
- Safety sensors are reliable
- Power supply is stable

## Dependencies

### External Systems
- Network infrastructure
- Power supply
- Facility emergency systems

### Regulatory
- Compliance with applicable standards
- Operating permits obtained
- Insurance requirements met

## Limitations

### Known Limitations
- Maximum safe speed: 2.0 m/s
- Workspace boundaries must be enforced
- Payload limits must not be exceeded
- Environmental limits must be maintained

### Hazards Outside Scope
- Acts of God (earthquakes, floods)
- Intentional sabotage
- Catastrophic facility failures
